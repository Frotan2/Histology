# Out-of-Scope Topics (Reference Only)

This directory contains brief reference notes on Junqueira 17th chapters that fall **outside** the official 16-chapter examination syllabus for the Afghanistan 1405 Specialty Examination.

**Per official syllabus rule:** All histology study materials should be based primarily on Junqueira 17th Edition and should remain within the official 16-chapter syllabus. Chapters not included should not be incorporated into the core study plan unless specifically required.

The following Junqueira chapters are out of core:

| Junqueira Ch | Topic | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Cartilage | Out of scope (specialised CT, type II collagen-rich) |
| 8 | Bone | Out of scope (mineralised CT, type I collagen + hydroxyapatite) |
| 10 | Muscle Tissue | Out of scope (skeletal, cardiac, smooth — covered briefly in Chapter 8 heart context and Chapter 11 conduction) |
| 19 | Urinary System | Out of scope (glomerular filtration barrier high-yield elsewhere) |
| 21 | Male Reproductive | Out of scope |
| 22 | Female Reproductive | Out of scope |
| 23 | Eye and Ear | Out of scope |

Cartilage and bone are specialised connective tissues (J7 and J8); they share the cell–matrix principle taught in Chapter 5 and are omitted from core to remain within syllabus.

Muscle tissue (J10) appears in core as part of the heart (Chapter 8, myocardium and conduction system) but is not treated as a standalone chapter.

The urinary filtration barrier (J19) is a high-yield application of basement membrane biology (J4 / Chapter 4) but is not in core.

Reproductive and eye/ear histology are outside the 16-chapter scope.
