## Depth Allocation Rationale (Per Official Syllabus)

Allocate depth by:
1. Syllabus relevance (Very High = more pages) — only 16 official chapters, all Very High/High
2. Conceptual importance (framework tissues = more depth)
3. Exam importance (epithelium classification, blood, pituitary, GI regional differences, lymphoid zones = high yield)
4. Likelihood of confusion (artery vs vein, bronchus vs bronchiole, duodenum vs jejunum vs ileum, thyroid vs parathyroid = needs Compare & Distinguish)
5. Clinical relevance (Marfan, Barrett, Goodpasture)
6. Dependency (methods → cell biology → tissues → organ systems)

Resulting page allocation after realignment:
- Methods, Epithelium, Connective, Nerve, Circulatory, Immune, Digestive, Endocrine = deepest
- Cytoplasm, Nucleus, Adipose, Blood, Hemopoiesis, Respiratory, Skin = medium
- Cartilage, Bone, Muscle, Urinary, Reproductive, Eye/Ear = out of scope, archived, not in core study plan per official rule
