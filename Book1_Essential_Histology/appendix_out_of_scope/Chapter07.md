# Chapter 7 — Muscle Tissue

### Opening Question
How do three tissues that all contract end up looking so different — and why does that matter for heart failure and gut motility?

### Why This Matters
Myocardial infarction loses intercalated discs, skeletal has satellite stem cells, smooth hyperplasia causes asthma hypertension. T-tubule position distinguishes skeletal vs cardiac.

### Learning Objectives
1. Compare skeletal cardiac smooth by striation nuclei number/position intercalated discs T-tubule position SR regeneration innervation.
2. Describe sarcomere Z disc M line A band I band H zone and contraction actin-myosin.
3. Explain why cardiac has intercalated disc three junctions and skeletal does not.
4. Predict functional consequence smooth dense bodies gap junctions.

### Big Picture
Muscle = actin-myosin contractile apparatus but arrangement junctions innervation determine speed endurance control.

### Core Concept
**Striation = sarcomeres, branching + intercalated disc = cardiac, no striation + dense bodies = smooth.**

> **CORE IDEA:** Skeletal voluntary fast strong satellite stem, cardiac involuntary branching intercalated disc gap syncytium, smooth involuntary slow sustained gap dense bodies.

### Build the Concept
**Skeletal:** long multinucleated syncytium myoblast fusion peripheral nuclei striated A dark myosin+actin I light actin only Z disc α-actinin M line myomesin sarcomere Z→Z functional unit T-tubule at A-I junction + 2 terminal cisternae triad SR Ca2+ release satellite cells stem between basal lamina and plasma membrane regeneration limited voluntary somatic motor NMJ acetylcholine.

**Cardiac:** branching cells single central nucleus sometimes 2 striated branching intercalated disc fascia adherens cadherin actin + desmosome desmoglein keratin + gap connexin electrical coupling T-tubule at Z disc + 1 terminal cisterna diad mitochondria abundant lipofuscin no satellite limited regeneration fibrosis after MI involuntary autonomic intercalated disc syncytium.

**Smooth:** spindle single central nucleus no striation no sarcomere dense bodies α-actinin + intermediate filaments desmin/vimentin anchor actin caveolae Ca2+ entry gap junctions coordinate slow sustained contraction calmodulin not troponin regeneration via mitosis involuntary autonomic + hormones.

### Structure → Function
Why skeletal triad at A-I vs cardiac diad at Z? Skeletal needs rapid Ca2+ release along whole fiber fast twitch → T-tubule at A-I where myosin heads. Cardiac needs Ca2+-induced Ca2+ release synchrony → T-tubule at Z with diad.

Why intercalated disc three junctions? Fascia adherens transmits force actin, desmosome resists shear intermediate filament, gap spreads depolarization → heart syncytium.

### Compare & Distinguish
| Feature | Skeletal | Cardiac | Smooth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Striation | Yes | Yes branching | No |
| Nuclei | Many peripheral | Single central 1-2 | Single central |
| Intercalated disc | No | Yes fascia+desmosome+gap | No |
| T-tubule | A-I junction triad 2 SR | Z disc diad 1 SR | No T caveolae |
| SR | Well developed | Moderate | Scant caveolae Ca2+ |
| Regeneration | Satellite | Minimal fibrosis | Mitosis |
| Innervation | Voluntary somatic NMJ | Involuntary autonomic Purkinje | Involuntary autonomic hormones |

### Recognition Logic
**Look for...** cross-striation + peripheral nuclei + unbranched long fiber skeletal. **Then confirm...** branching + central nucleus + intercalated disc cardiac. Spindle + central nucleus + no striation smooth.

**Do not confuse with...** cardiac vs skeletal both striated but cardiac branching + intercalated disc + central. Smooth vs fibroblast both spindle but smooth no collagen and dense bodies.

**Decisive:** intercalated disc cardiac, peripheral multinucleated skeletal, dense bodies no striation smooth.

### Clinical Correlation
**MI:** loss blood myocyte death no regeneration no satellite → fibrosis collagen I scar intercalated disc disruption arrhythmia gap loss.

**Myasthenia gravis:** autoantibody acetylcholine receptor NMJ skeletal weakness.

**Asthma/hypertension:** smooth hyperplasia/hypertrophy hypercontractility.

### Common Misconceptions
**Misconception:** Cardiac many nuclei like skeletal. Reality single central sometimes binucleate not multinucleated peripheral.

**Misconception:** Smooth no actin-myosin. Reality has actin-myosin but no sarcomere anchored dense bodies slow sustained.
